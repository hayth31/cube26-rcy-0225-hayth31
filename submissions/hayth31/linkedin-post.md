🚀 Excited to share my Round 2 build for the Cube Buildathon by **@Sydon.AI** & **@CodeQuesters**!

I built the **Recovery Manager** (Step 5 of 5: Money Back) — an autonomous, evidence-driven fee dispute agent designed for ecommerce sellers and 3PL prep centers.

### The Problem:
Amazon FBA charges fees weeks after inventory leaves the warehouse — from unplanned prep penalties and dimensional weight tier misclassifications to lost inbound inventory. Sellers struggle to dispute these because operational proof is scattered across disparate systems. Most tools either file aggressive, unsupported claims that risk seller account standing, or require tedious manual paperwork.

### What I Built:
Recovery Manager works without cameras, turning structured records from upstream managers (Receiving, Prep, Pack, and Returns) into audit-proof claims:
✅ **Authoritative Policy Grounding:** Matches every fee against published Amazon FBA policies and statutory dispute windows.  
✅ **Strict Multi-Tenant Row-Level Security:** Zero cross-tenant data leakage between fulfillment organizations.  
✅ **Conservative Decision Engine:** Classifies charges into `CONTRADICTED`, `SUPPORTED`, `SILENT`, and `UNCERTAIN` — zero invented evidence.  
✅ **Batched & Fail-Open AI:** Powered by Google Gemini 2.0 Flash (Free Tier) with seamless offline fallback.  
✅ **100% Claim Precision:** 0 false claims on benchmark testing, protecting seller account health.
