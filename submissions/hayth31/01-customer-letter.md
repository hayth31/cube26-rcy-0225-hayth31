### Why You Haven't Fought Back
Today, fighting Amazon feels like a losing battle:
1. **The 6-Week Latency Gap:** The fee appears weeks after the shipment left your facility. By then, the cardboard is recycled, the pallet is gone, and nobody remembers who taped the box.
2. **The "Guilty Until Proven Innocent" Trap:** Amazon Seller Support demands proof: photos of the original packaging, Bill of Lading timestamps, certified scale logs, and prep compliance records. You don't have them in one place.
3. **The Standing Risk:** If you hire a generic agency or use a bot that files blanket, aggressive disputes, Amazon flags your account for abusive claims. Filing a claim you cannot prove puts your entire selling privilege at risk.

### The Solution: Recovery Manager
Recovery Manager changes the equation by closing the loop between the warehouse floor and your financial ledger.

It does not rely on cameras at your desk. Instead, it acts as your automated Chief Dispute Officer:
1. **It Ingests Channel Reports:** Reading fee reports, inventory adjustments, and reimbursement reports as soon as they post.
2. **It Follows the Unit's Physical Journey:** It instantly queries the structured evidence left by the upstream managers:
   - **Receiving Manager:** Proving your goods arrived intact and uncrushed at the dock.
   - **Prep Manager:** Proving that the polybag was sealed, the suffocation warning was legible, and the FNSKU was placed flat over the original barcode.
   - **Pack Manager:** Providing certified weight and dimension timestamps.
   - **Returns Manager:** Documenting physical return intake and disposition.
3. **It Knows Authoritative Amazon Rules:** It cross-references the fee against Amazon’s published FBA policies (Inbound Performance Feedback, Cubiscan Discrepancies, 45-Day Return Restitution, and Lost Inventory Reimbursements).

   - **UNCERTAIN:** If the barcode was blurry or the inspector was unsure, the case is flagged for human review. **We never invent evidence.**
