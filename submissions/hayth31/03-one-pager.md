# 03 · One-Pager: Recovery Manager

**Product:** Recovery Manager (`hayth31`)  
**Stream:** Commerce Context · Step 5 of 5 (Money Back)  
**Target User:** Amazon FBA Brands, Aggregators, 3PL Fulfillment Centers  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Amazon sellers lose 3%–7% of gross revenue to misapplied fulfillment fees, uncredited inbound losses, and erroneous defect charges. Legitimate claims go unfiled because charges appear 4–8 weeks after physical fulfillment when proof is scattered. Recovery Manager ingests channel fee reports, cross-matches individual charges with upstream evidence generated by Receiving, Prep, Pack, and Returns Managers, and produces audit-proof claims grounded in official Amazon policies.

## 3. Kill Condition

> **Hard Kill Condition:**  
> If the autonomous agent's audited Claim Precision drops below **90%** over any 30-day window (i.e. more than 1 in 10 recommended disputes is rejected by Amazon as unfounded or fraudulent), **the autonomous auto-claim pipeline is immediately revoked**, and the agent is demoted to a passive `pending_review` recommendation state requiring mandatory warehouse supervisor authorization before submission.
